Bert Sie

Co-Founder · Managing Director

Bert co-developed the “Double U” concept with Teresa in early 2022, focusing on go-to-market and growth strategies for technology-driven companies. He currently serves as Head of Sales & Business Development for BeammWave and AlixLabs in Taiwan.

Bert began his career as a Sales Engineer at EDOM, a top-7 global IC distributor, and later advanced into senior leadership roles, founding the company’s first mass-market business group and leading sales and marketing initiatives. Earlier in his career, he managed product teams across multiple applications and supported five startups from 0 to 1.

He holds an MBA from National Central University, Taiwan.

Teresa Jhan

Teresa is a seasoned professional with over 17 years of experience in the IoT and high-tech industry, possessing deep expertise in end-to-end solutions spanning cloud platforms, devices, and key components. Her ability to align technology with real business needs makes her a key contributor to the Double U team.

Fred Chen

VP Channel & Distribution

Fred brings more than 22 years of experience in the IC supply chain. Most recently, he led Realtek's engagements with the largest PC/NB brand in China.

Before Realtek, he held a pivotal role in channel sales for the Taiwan district at Micron. Fred began his career at Avnet, where he honed his skills in product management. He holds a BS in Electrical Engineering from the National Taipei University of Technology in Taiwan.

Double U (DoubleU-Tech Advisory) is the go-to-market and business development partner for tech innovators entering Taiwan and APAC.
