APS™ demonstrated on UMC 300 mm wafers

How a Swedish semiconductor start-up engaged Taiwan's foundries and semiconductor supply chain.

Client: AlixLabs · Sweden Sector: Semiconductor process Milestone: 2025
01

The challenge

AlixLabs' Atomic Layer Etch Pitch Splitting (APS™) lowers patterning cost at advanced nodes. For a new process technology, the decisive step is showing it works on production-grade wafers — inside the foundry ecosystem.

Much of that ecosystem is in Taiwan, and getting a start-up's technology in front of it takes local relationships and credibility.

02

Our approach

Double U acted as AlixLabs' Taiwan sales and business development lead:

  • Engaged Taiwan's foundries on behalf of AlixLabs
  • Built relationships across Taiwan's semiconductor supply chain
03

The result

APS™ was demonstrated on UMC 300 mm wafers, presented at the 2025 CMC Conference. AlixLabs' APS patents have been granted in the US, EU and Taiwan.

Source: AlixLabs press release
