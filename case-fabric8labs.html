ECAM liquid cooling in mass production in 2026

How a US cooling innovator reached Taiwan's AI server ODM ecosystem.

Client: Fabric8Labs · USA Sector: AI data center / thermal Milestone: 2026
01

The challenge

Fabric8Labs uses electrochemical additive manufacturing (ECAM) to print high-performance metal cold plates for liquid-cooled AI data centers.

The AI servers those cold plates go into are designed and built largely by Taiwan's server ODMs — so winning in AI cooling means winning in Taiwan.

02

Our approach

Double U worked as Fabric8Labs' business accelerator in Taiwan:

  • Connected Fabric8Labs with Taiwan's AI server ODM ecosystem
  • Created trade-show exposure in front of the right industry audience
03

The result

ECAM cold plates are in mass production and shipping with GIGABYTE, powering leading liquid-cooled AI data centers.
