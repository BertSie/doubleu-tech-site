Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

DoubleU-Tech Advisory ("Double U", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you submit the contact form, we collect the information you provide:

  • Your name and email address
  • The subject and content of your message

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect payment information on this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you send us only to respond to your inquiry and to follow up on a potential business relationship. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Service Providers

  • Cloudflare hosts this website and provides Turnstile, a security check that helps protect our contact form from spam and abuse.
  • Google Forms stores the messages submitted through our contact form.
  • Google Fonts delivers the fonts used on this website.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

We keep inquiry messages only as long as needed to respond and to maintain our business records.

Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you have sent us. To make a request, please contact us through the contact form.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
