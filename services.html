Engagement Model

You commit step by step — each phase begins only when your project reaches the next milestone.

PHASE 1

PHASE 2

Commercialization

Design-in, samples, negotiation and contract support, and production tracking.

Each phase is confirmed in writing with a mutually agreed contract, and terms are renewable at expiry.
